SPUR Privacy Policy Last Updated: October 2026 This Privacy Policy explains how SPUR collects, uses, and protects your personal information in compliance with Canada’s Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA), British Columbia’s Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA), and Quebec’s Law 25. 1. Information We Collect To facilitate our concierge routing, we collect only the information strictly necessary for the service: Identity Data: Your first name. Contact Data: Your 10-digit mobile phone number. Location Data: Your selected city and neighborhood (e.g., Vancouver, Montreal). Service Preferences: Your specific specialist requests and timing preferences. 2. Purpose of Collection and Consent We collect your information to match you with a grooming specialist, coordinate your arrival, and provide SMS/WhatsApp dispatch updates. Under Quebec's Law 25, consent must be granular and requested for each specific purpose. By submitting a dispatch request and affirmatively continuing past our consent disclaimer, you provide express consent for SPUR to use your mobile number specifically for these logistical communications. 3. Communication and Anti-Spam Compliance (CASL) Your phone number is used to initiate WhatsApp or SMS notifications strictly related to your active dispatch request. You may opt out of future communications at any time by replying "STOP" or messaging the SPUR Concierge directly. 4. Sharing of Information SPUR never sells your personal data. We only communicate your first name, estimated arrival time, and chosen service context to the specific independent specialist who accepts your dispatch request. No other third parties receive your personal information unless required by law. 5. Data Retention and Security We retain your personal information only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected or to maintain your seamless returning-customer experience. We utilize local browser storage (localStorage) to remember your details to reduce future booking friction. You may clear this data at any time via your browser settings. 6. Your Privacy Rights Depending on your jurisdiction, including protections under Quebec's Law 25 and PIPEDA, you maintain the right to: Access the personal information SPUR holds about you. Request the rectification of inaccurate or incomplete information. Withdraw your consent for data processing or request the erasure of your profile. 7.
